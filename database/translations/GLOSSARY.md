# Translation Glossary (TH / EN / 中文)

Fixed renderings for DB content (`database/translations/*.json`) and the site lang files
(`lang/{th,en,zh}`). Keep both in agreement.

## Rules

- Product, category and subcategory names: keep Latin-script segments, model codes, sizes
  and units verbatim; translate only Thai segments. `H-Beam - เหล็กเอชบีม ใหม่เก่าเก็บ` →
  en `H-Beam - H-Beam Steel, New Old Stock`, zh `H-Beam - H型钢 全新库存`.
- Numbers, prices, phone numbers, emails, URLs, standards (TIS / มอก., ISO), brand names and
  customer/partner company names stay verbatim (Latin script).
- Company: `Wongpanit Recycle Rayong Co., Ltd.` in en and zh; short form `WPN`.
- Simplified Chinese, mainland usage, full-width punctuation in Chinese sentences
  (，。：；（）), half-width inside Latin segments.
